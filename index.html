FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need. A simple one-page site costs less than a larger site with custom features. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll give you a fixed quote before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly content such as text and photos is ready. You get a clear timeline with your quote.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You review the design before I build it, and revision rounds are included and agreed up front.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. I can set up your domain name and hosting for you, or work with what you already have.

What do you need from me to start?

A short chat about your business, your logo if you have one, and any text or photos you want to use. If you don't have these yet, I can help.
